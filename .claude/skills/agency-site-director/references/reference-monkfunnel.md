# Reference analysis — monkfunnel.com (user-supplied, reviewed 2026-09-01)

## What they are
Conversion-first website studio for startups, Delhi, India. Positioning: "Your website should be your best salesperson… one outcome, one message, one action. Shipped in 21 days."

## Their visual identity (do NOT copy)
- Warm cream/off-white palette, classical display serif with italic emphasis words + sans body.
- Pixel-art game world: 8-bit mascot (bottom-right, cycles witty tooltips: "Strategy without execution is just a slide deck", "You talk to the founders. Not an account manager", "Your conversion ceiling is usually a copy problem, not a traffic one"), pixel sky/trees/grass section backgrounds, pixel clouds.
- Rounded pill nav that floats and expands on scroll; browser-frame mockup inside hero.

## Their conversion skeleton (DO learn from this)
1. Hero: serif headline with italic key phrase + 3-line subcopy + dual CTA (dark "Book Intro Call" + white WhatsApp "Send Message") + product mockup.
2. Services: ONE "Primary Service" card (website build) + 4 secondary growth cards (SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads, Funnels) under "when you're ready to scale traffic" — clear anchor offer, upsell path.
3. Lead magnet: "Get a free homepage teardown →" (low-friction, high-value).
4. Process: numbered editorial list 01 Discover / 02 Wireframe / 03 Design / 04 Build, with a promise chip ("Brief to live in 21 days — every time").
5. Why-us trio: "You approve every move / One outcome, not ten / Shipped in 21 days".
6. Pricing: "Start with the website. Scale from there." — every tier starts with the site then adds channels.
7. Testimonials: 3 cards, founder name + role + city, each with a metric chip ("3× more demo bookings", "Cart abandonment down 28%"), middle card dark for emphasis.
8. Tech marquee: Next.js, Tailwind, Figma, Framer Motion, Vercel, TypeScript, Notion.
9. FAQ accordion; final CTA with reassurance microcopy ("No pitch, no obligation — just 30 useful minutes").
10. Footer: tagline, Company/Legal/Contact columns, email + +91 phone, WhatsApp button persistent in nav.

## Verdict for our build
Steal the skeleton (anchor offer → scale path, metric-chip proof, promise chip, teardown lead magnet, WhatsApp-first for India, reassurance microcopy). Reject the skin (pixel world, cream/pastel, cute mascot) — our identity must be the opposite pole so it reads unmistakably different: dark, cinematic, signal/lab aesthetic. See `concept-signal.md`.
